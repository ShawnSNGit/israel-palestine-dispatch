# STEMpathize principles

STEMpathize is a design philosophy and set of practices for producing data-driven, empathy-sensitive reporting and synthesis. It combines rigorous quantitative checks with explicit sensitivity and contextualization when human impact is involved.

Core principles
- Rigour: every extractable claim should be tied to snapshots and canonicalized representations.
- Reproducibility: digests must be reconstructable from stored snapshots and recorded model inputs.
- Empathy & sensitivity: when reporting on human harm, displacement, casualties, or trauma, include contextualization, avoid sensational language, and prioritize human dignity.
- Hedging: where sources conflict, use explicit hedging templates and attribute claims clearly.
- Transparency: publish methodology, heuristics, and limitations alongside digests.

Practical steps for writers & editors
- When a cluster is flagged, include direct citations and the snapshot hashes in the report.
- Use the suggested STEMpathize hedging language and expand it with quotes from primary sources where appropriate.
- If legal questions arise, route to the legal-review queue before publication.
