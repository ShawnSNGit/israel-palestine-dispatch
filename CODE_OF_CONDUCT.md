# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:
- Harassment and exclusionary behavior

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainers.

For the full text, see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
