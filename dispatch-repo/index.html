On "live": this page tries a real refresh on load and on demand — it calls each outlet's public RSS feed through a third-party feed-reader proxy (rss2json.com), since browsers can't fetch most news sites directly. When that succeeds, a column's status dot turns green and its headlines are pulled straight from the feed. When a feed has no public RSS, or the proxy is down or rate-limited, the dot turns amber/red and the column quietly falls back to the snapshot above — it never fails silently into stale data pretending to be fresh. Headlines are paraphrased in the static snapshot; live-fetched ones show the outlet's own published title, as any feed reader would.
